---
title: Manage feedback
description: Review, favorite and delete the feedback you receive.
---

Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and select a product to see all of its feedback, with each customer's message, rating, name and photo.

## Favorite feedback

Click the **heart** on a piece of feedback to favorite it. Click it again to remove it from your favorites.

Only favorited feedback appears in the [embedded widget](/docs/embed-widget), so favoriting is how you choose what visitors to your site see.

## Delete feedback

Click the **delete** icon on a piece of feedback and confirm. Use this for spam or anything you don't want to keep.

<Callout type="warn">Deleted feedback can't be recovered.</Callout>

Only you can favorite or delete feedback on your products.
