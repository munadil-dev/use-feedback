## Share the link

Every product has a public feedback link in this format:

```txt
https://vouch.munadil.com/YOUR_PRODUCT_ID
```

You can copy it right after creating the product, or later from the product's page in the dashboard with **Copy link**, or under **Share and embed**. Share it anywhere: email, social media, a QR code, a thank-you page.

## What customers fill in

| Field             | Required | Notes                                                    |
| ----------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Message           | Yes      | Their feedback.                                          |
| Your name         | Yes      | Shown on the widget.                                     |
| Your email        | Yes      | Must be a valid email. Never shown publicly.             |
| Upload your photo | No       | Shown on the widget. A default avatar is used otherwise. |
| Rate              | Yes      | 1 to 5 stars. Starts at 3.                               |

Customers don't need an account. The form checks every field before sending, shows errors next to the fields that need fixing, and works with a keyboard and screen readers.

After submitting, customers see a confirmation page.
