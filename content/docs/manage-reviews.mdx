---
title: Manage reviews
description: Favorite and delete the reviews you receive.
---

Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and select a product to see all of its reviews, with each customer's name, email, rating, message and the date it was sent.

## Favorite reviews

Click the **heart** on a review to favorite it. Click it again to remove it from your favorites.

Only favorited reviews appear in the [embedded widget](/docs/embed-widget), so favoriting is how you choose what visitors to your site see.

## Delete reviews

Click the **delete** icon on a review and confirm. Use this for spam or anything you don't want to keep.

<Callout type="warn">Deleted reviews can't be recovered.</Callout>

Only you can favorite or delete reviews on your products.
