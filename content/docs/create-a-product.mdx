---
title: Create a product
description: Set up a product to start collecting reviews for it.
---

A product is anything you want reviews for: an app, a service, a course. Each product gets its own review form, link and widget.

## Create one

1. Sign in and open the [dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Click **New product**.
3. Fill in the form and click **Create product**. The preview next to the form shows what customers will see.

| Field            | Shown to customers   | Example                                                     |
| ---------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Product name** | No                   | Acme                                                        |
| **Page title**   | Yes, as the heading  | How are you finding Acme?                                   |
| **Message**      | Yes, under the title | We read every reply. Tell us what you love and what to fix. |

The product name identifies the product in your dashboard. The page title and message are what customers see at the top of the review form.

After submitting, you get the product's review link with a **Copy link** button.

## Delete a product

Open the menu (•••) on the product's card in the dashboard and delete it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a product also deletes all of its reviews. This can't be undone.
</Callout>
