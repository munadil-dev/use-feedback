---
title: Create a product
description: Set up a product to start collecting feedback for it.
---

A product is anything you want feedback on: an app, a service, a course. Each product gets its own feedback form, link and widget.

## Create one

1. Sign in and open the [dashboard](/dashboard).
2. Click **New product**.
3. Fill in the form and click **Create product**. The preview next to the form shows what customers will see.

| Field              | Shown to customers   | Example                        |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Product name**   | No                   | Blog App                       |
| **Title**          | Yes, as the heading  | Blog App review                |
| **Custom message** | Yes, under the title | Review my blog app which has … |

The product name identifies the product in your dashboard. The title and custom message are what customers see at the top of the feedback form.

After submitting, you get the product's feedback link with a **Copy link** button.

## Delete a product

Open the menu (•••) on the product's card in the dashboard and delete it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a product also deletes all of its feedback. This can't be undone.
</Callout>
