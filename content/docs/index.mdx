Vouch turns what your customers say into social proof. Every product you create gets a public review form and an embeddable widget, so you can collect reviews and show the best ones on your site. You don't need hosting, a form builder or any code beyond two lines of HTML.

## Who it's for

- **Indie makers and small teams** who want testimonials on their landing page without building a backend.
- **Freelancers and agencies** collecting client reviews for a portfolio.
- **Anyone with a website** who can paste HTML, whether it runs on WordPress, Webflow, Framer or a custom React app.

## How it works

1. **Create a product** in your dashboard. You get a public review link.
2. **Share the link** with your customers. They leave a message, a 1–5 star rating and an optional photo. They don't need an account.
3. **Check reviews** in the dashboard. Favorite the reviews you like and delete spam.
4. **Embed the widget** on your website. It shows only the reviews you favorited.

```html title="Your website"
<div id="embed-reviews"></div>
<script src="https://vouch.munadil.com/api/embed-reviews?productId=YOUR_PRODUCT_ID"></script>
```

## You control what's public

Nothing a customer submits appears on your site until you favorite it. Unfavorite a review to hide it again, or delete it to remove it for good. Only you can favorite or delete reviews for your products.
