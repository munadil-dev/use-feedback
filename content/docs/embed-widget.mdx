---
title: Embed the widget
description: Show your favorited reviews on any website with one script tag.
---

## Add it to your site

Open your product in the [dashboard](/dashboard) and copy the embed code. It looks like this:

```html
<div id="embed-reviews"></div>
<script src="https://vouch.munadil.com/api/embed-reviews?productId=YOUR_PRODUCT_ID"></script>
```

Paste it into your site's HTML where the reviews should appear. The widget fills the `div` with a card for each favorited review.

It works on any site that lets you add HTML: plain HTML, WordPress, Webflow, Framer, or a React/Vue/Svelte app. The script is plain JavaScript with no dependencies and no iframe.

## What it shows

- Only reviews you [favorited](/docs/manage-reviews#favorite-reviews).
- Each customer's name, photo (or a default avatar), star rating and message.
- Nothing at all if you haven't favorited any reviews yet. The `div` stays empty and takes up no space.

## Good to know

<Callout title="Updates can take up to 2 minutes">
  The widget is cached for speed. After you favorite or unfavorite a review, it
  can take up to 2 minutes to show on your site.
</Callout>

- **One widget per page.** The script looks for the element with `id="embed-reviews"`, so add one per page.
- **Place the `div` before the script.** The script fills the `div` as soon as it loads, so the `div` has to exist by then.
- **Accessible.** Cards are announced as a list, ratings are read as "Rated 4 out of 5", and photos load lazily.
- **Safe.** Reviews are inserted as text, never as HTML, so a customer can't inject code into your site.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing shows up.** Check that you favorited at least one review, that the `productId` in the script URL matches your product, and that the `div` comes before the `<script>`.

**My change isn't showing.** Wait up to 2 minutes for the cache to refresh, then reload the page.
