By the end of this guide, your website shows real customer feedback that you picked.

## Before you start

You need:

- A Google account to sign in.
- A website where you can add HTML. Any site builder or framework works.

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in

Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and sign in with your Google account.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a product

Click **+ Create a new product** and fill in the name, title and custom message. The title and message are what customers see on the feedback form, so write them as a question or a short thank-you. See [Create a product](/docs/create-a-product) for what each field does.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the feedback link

When the product is created, click **Copy to clipboard** to copy its feedback link. It looks like this:

```txt
https://usefeedback.munadil.com/YOUR_PRODUCT_ID
```

Send it to your customers by email, on social media or on a thank-you page. You can find it again later under **Feedback URL** on the product's page. See [Collect feedback](/docs/collect-feedback).

<Callout title="Try it yourself first">
  Open the link in a private window and submit a test review. You'll see the
  whole flow your customers go through, and you'll have something to embed right
  away. Delete it later from the dashboard.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Favorite the best feedback

Open the product in your dashboard and click the heart on the feedback you want to show publicly. Only favorited feedback appears on your website. See [Manage feedback](/docs/manage-feedback).

</Step>
<Step>

### Embed the widget

Copy the embed code from the product's page and paste it into your website's HTML where the feedback should appear:

```html
<div id="embed-feedbacks"></div>
<script src="https://usefeedback.munadil.com/api/embed-feedbacks?productId=YOUR_PRODUCT_ID"></script>
```

See [Embed the widget](/docs/embed-widget) for platform tips.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

Reload your website. You should see a card for each favorited review.

If the widget is empty:

- Make sure at least one review is favorited.
- Wait up to 2 minutes. The widget is cached, so new favorites take a moment to appear.
- Check that the `productId` in the script matches your product.

See [troubleshooting](/docs/embed-widget#troubleshooting) for more.
