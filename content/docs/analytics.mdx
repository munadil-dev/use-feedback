---
title: Analytics
description: See how many people open your review link and how many leave a review.
---

Open the [dashboard](/dashboard) and select a product. The **Analytics** panel at the top shows how your review link is doing.

## Views, responses and conversion

| Number         | What it counts                                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Views**      | Visits to your review link.                                        |
| **Responses**  | Reviews submitted.                                                 |
| **Conversion** | Responses as a share of views, so how many visitors left a review. |

A view is counted once per browser session, so refreshing the page doesn't inflate it. Views of your own link count too.

Pick **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days** to change the period. The chart shows views and responses per day, with days in UTC.

## Ratings

Under the chart, **Ratings** shows the average star rating and how many reviews got each rating, from 5 stars down to 1. This covers all reviews, not just the selected period.
